Selected Projects

Mobile Steel Hoist

Structural Design • Welding • Hand Calculations

Designed and fabricated a mobile lifting system with 600 lb capacity using salvaged steel. Conducted structural analysis using AISC manual to validate safety factors and developed solutions for material constraints including beveling, rigging, and weld sequencing.

MIG Welding Statics Analysis Scrap Fabrication

Two-Tier Cantilever Computer Desk

Structural Design • Fabrication • Problem Solving

Built a two-tier cantilever desk from salvaged materials to meet specific spatial constraints. Addressed thermal warping during welding through mechanical correction using a car jack, and developed integrated cable management solutions mid-build.

Welding Woodworking Thermal Management

East Campus Fort and Coaster, 2025 REX

Woodworking • Structural Construction • Lighting

Along with a small team of the initial designers, spent 15+ hours a day for two weeks building a 3 story fort with LED displays, sound, and lighting, as well as a roller coaster (of which unfortunately was not completed due to premitting issues).

Wood Fabrication Lighting

Silicon Solar Cell Design & Testing

Micro/Nano Processing • Device Fabrication • Characterization

Designed, fabricated, and tested silicon solar cells. Optimized finger geometry, metal coverage, and ARC thickness to improve efficiency, while diagnosing anomalous wafers and process defects.

Semiconductors Lithography I–V Characterization

Carbon Fiber Laminate Flexural Study

MIT • Composites • Experimental Mechanics

Investigated fiber orientation effects on flexural modulus using hand layup laminates and ASTM D790 three-point bending tests. Compared results to classical laminate theory predictions.

Composites ASTM D790 Classical Laminate Theory

Electrochemical Carbon Capture - Team Rahimi

University of Houston Research • Materials Development • Process Optimization

Developed membraneless electrochemical cells for CO₂ capture from point sources and seawater. Optimized electrode materials and utilized stepwise polarization to help refine EMAR techniques for industrial energy efficiency.

Electrochemistry Material Characterization Process Design

CEREBRUZ: Lunar Polymer Recycling System (NASA)

NASA-Funded Research • Materials Testing • Manufacturing

Designed and optimized injection molding system for recycling lunar waste polymers (thermoset Zotek F30 foam) into functional composites. Conducted tensile testing, SEM, partical size analysis, FTIR, and hardness testing to evaluate processing parameters and mechanical properties.

Injection Molding Material Testing NASA LunaRecycle

Granular Jamming & Coral Reef Restoration

MIT Self-Assembly Lab • DARPA BRACE • Parametric Design

Developed tension-based granular jamming systems for rapid infrastructure deployment (DARPA) and designed modular geometric structures for coral reef restoration in Maldives. Conducted physical prototyping, mechanical testing, and parametric design optimization.
